# Colony (the team)

Who works in the hive. `roster.yaml` lists every bee; each role has one job description here, named after its key.

## How the team works

- **Nicknames.** Bees are called by a nickname from a real hive job, usually `<word> <caste>`. A caste is a department and can hold several roles.
- **Pairs.** A main role is a pair. The bee on Claude takes design, hard problems and decisions within the discipline, at raised effort, never max. Its `Bumble` twin on Codex takes routine volume work. Each reviews the other's work.
- **Evaluation roles.** Guards, the Hornet and the Scout are set to an engine per piece of work, by balance between the engines.
- **Signing.** Every output is signed `Nickname · Title · engine`.
- **Getting help.** A bee that can do the work does it. If it can't, it asks Queen for a worker, not for the work. Queen sends a Symbiont from the pool, or drafts a new one for the owner's OK. The bee works with the Symbiont and stays responsible for the result.

Symbionts are one-task roles. They stay in the pool for reuse, but they are not part of the hive, so they aren't listed here.
