# Learning

Sessions where the owner learns: new tools, mentoring, awareness. It is org-wide and has no sprint.

1. The Nurse spots what the owner needs to learn, for example a tool the team has just chosen.
2. The Nurse asks Queen for a worker, and Queen sends a Symbiont.
3. The owner sets the session. The Symbiont teaches, with the Nurse alongside.
4. The Nurse logs the session and raises any follow-up tasks.
