# Delivery

Doing the work: code, assets, data, docs. The owner sets the goal and gives the final word.

Delivery runs in strict one-week sprints, because this work should keep pace. Sprints start when a project's Delivery starts, not before.

## A sprint

- Sprint planning sets the dates and an ordered list of ready tickets within capacity.
- The end date is fixed. Unfinished tickets carry over and are counted, not hidden.
- When a sprint ends, its review, retro and next planning are due at the next session.

## A ticket

1. A ticket enters a sprint only when it's ready: Discovery has settled what it must do.
2. The named doer works on a `cell/` branch.
3. Named reviewers run the Gaps round and, if needed, the Confirm round (`review.md`).
4. The owner gives the word, and the work is merged.

When the owner checks Delivery work, item by item or in batches, is decided when Delivery starts. Until then it is item by item.
