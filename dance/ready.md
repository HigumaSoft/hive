# Ready

When the owner says "ready", the Waggle Dancer:

1. Reads the org calendar for meetings due or overdue.
2. Lists what is waiting: paused work and open flags.
3. If something is due, runs it in order: urgent meetings, then regular ones, then consultations. A meeting that depends on another waits for it.
4. If nothing is due, hands the owner to the Round Dancer, who takes the next ticket.

A sprint that has ended makes its review, retro and planning due.
